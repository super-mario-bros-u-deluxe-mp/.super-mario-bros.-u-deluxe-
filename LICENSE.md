## 06 — EXPLORE THE ENVIRONMENTS


The Mushroom Kingdom is divided into distinct biomes, each presenting unique hazards and aesthetic themes.


*   **Acorn Plains:** The classic starting point for your journey.
*   **Layer-Cake Desert:** Shifting sands and treacherous platforming.
*   **Frosted Glacier:** Slippery surfaces requiring precise momentum.
*   **Soda Jungle:** Dense, mysterious, and filled with hidden secrets.
